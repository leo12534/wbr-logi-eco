Choose Your Experience

20-Minute Stand-Alone Sessions

Fast-paced plenary sessions pairing one vendor with one pharma professional for a focused, balanced discussion, designed to keep attention high.

Guest Speaker

An external expert delivers an independent, thought-provoking talk. This format brings fresh perspectives and high-credibility insights to the stage.

Ask Me (Just About) Anything

A pharma professional interviews a vendor live on stage, exploring personal stories and bold questions. Entertaining, authentic, and interactive, with audience Q&A adding energy and unpredictability.

Passion Project

Senior pharma professionals share personal passion projects that matter beyond their day job. These sessions blend storytelling, reflection, and audience interaction.

Word Cloud

Live questions are posed to the audience, with real-time responses displayed as a word cloud. This interactive format sparks discussion, reveals collective insights, and keeps the room fully engaged.

Pecha Kucha

A dynamic, image-only presentation: 20 slides, 20 seconds per slide. Fast, creative, and highly visual, followed by a reflective discussion to deepen the insights.

30-Minute Group Sessions

Curated plenary discussions led by a facilitator. Tightly managed to encourage debate and diverse perspectives while keeping energy and focus high.

Town Hall

Audience-driven Q&A where participants submit questions directly to the panel. Creates open dialogue, real engagement, and a sense of shared ownership of the conversation.

Chat Show

Three guests take the stage one by one for short, informal interviews around their passion projects. Blends storytelling with spontaneity, ending in a group discussion or audience Q&A.

Quick-Fire Questions

Audience members vote on pre-set questions, with the most popular put to the panel live. A high-energy format that surfaces what the audience really wants to know.

Interactive Masterclass

An informal, discussion-led session designed for deep participation rather than presentations. Small-group breakouts and open dialogue allow ideas to be shared, challenged, and refined.

Tech Demo Masterclass

A live demonstration of innovative technology, guided by a facilitator and pharma speaker. Combines practical demos with discussion and Q&A to ensure relevance, quality, and real learning.
